- **Item type**: Ability Gacha (random ability assignment for Custom Mochi)
- **Location**: "My Mochi" → Ability selection → "Random Roll"
- **Purchase**: Paid with in-game coins as part of Custom Mochi slot creation
- **Cost per roll**: 8,000 coins (included in Custom Mochi creation cost)
- **Direct paid-currency purchase**: No. Coins are earned through gameplay or purchased via separate coin packs.
- **Real-money cashout**: Not possible (no real gambling element)

Each gacha roll determines one of the following tiers, which controls the number of bonus abilities granted.

The Game's ability gacha has **no pity (guaranteed acquisition) or mileage system**. Each roll is independently determined according to the probabilities above.
